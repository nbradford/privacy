Privacy Policy

Typerighter · Last updated November 10, 2026

Typerighter does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app and keyboard make no network connections, so nothing you type leaves your device unless you choose to email it as feedback.

What you type

Everything you type stays on your device. Typerighter has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no server to send anything to.

Full Access

iOS lets you turn on Allow Full Access for keyboards. Typerighter uses it for two things, neither of which iOS allows without it: playing haptic feedback when you press a key, and saving text you flag as autocorrect feedback. It doesn't use Full Access to reach the network or read your clipboard, and the keyboard works fully with it turned off.

Settings

Your settings — autocorrect, swipe typing, and haptic strength — are stored on your device and shared between the app and the keyboard through an App Group. Deleting Typerighter deletes them.

Autocorrect feedback

If you select text and choose Share… › Typerighter, or tap Add to Feedback on the keyboard's suggestion bar, that text is saved on your device, in the same App Group as your settings. Nothing is saved unless you choose to, and the keyboard never offers it in password fields. You can review, delete, or clear saved text in the Typerighter app at any time, and deleting Typerighter deletes it.

Typerighter never sends this text itself. Send by Email opens your own mail app with the text, your Typerighter version, and your iOS version filled in, and you can edit it before sending. If you send it, we receive that email and your email address, and use them only to improve autocorrect and to reply to you. We don't share them with anyone, and you can ask us to delete them at the address below.

Text Replacements

Typerighter reads your iOS Text Replacements through Apple's keyboard API so it can expand them as you type. It doesn't copy, store, or send them anywhere.

Children

Typerighter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, at any age. The app itself collects no personal information; the only information we receive is feedback someone chooses to email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date at the top.

Contact
