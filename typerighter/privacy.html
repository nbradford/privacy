Privacy Policy

Typerighter · Last updated October 24, 2026

Typerighter does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app and keyboard make no network connections, so nothing you type ever leaves your device.

What you type

Everything you type stays on your device. Typerighter has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no server to send anything to.

Full Access

iOS lets you turn on Allow Full Access for keyboards. Typerighter uses it only to play haptic feedback when you press a key, because iOS doesn't allow a keyboard to vibrate without it. It doesn't use Full Access to reach the network or read your clipboard, and the keyboard works fully with it turned off.

Settings

Your settings — autocorrect, swipe typing, and haptic strength — are stored on your device and shared between the app and the keyboard through an App Group. Deleting Typerighter deletes them.

Text Replacements

Typerighter reads your iOS Text Replacements through Apple's keyboard API so it can expand them as you type. It doesn't copy, store, or send them anywhere.

Children

Typerighter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, at any age, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date at the top.
