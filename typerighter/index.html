A keyboard with no predictions, no learning, and no AI

Something not working, or not behaving the way you expected? Email stratum.cutout-6q@icloud.com and you'll get a reply from the person who wrote the app.

Turning it on

  1. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose Typerighter.
  3. Optional: tap Typerighter and turn on Allow Full Access for haptic feedback.
  4. In any text field, tap or hold the globe key to switch to it.

Common questions

  • No haptics? iOS only lets a keyboard vibrate with Allow Full Access turned on. Typerighter uses it for nothing else.
  • No key clicks? Clicks follow your iOS setting under Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Keyboard Feedback.
  • Changed a setting and nothing happened? Settings take effect the next time the keyboard opens.
  • Unwanted correction? Tap delete right after it to undo it, or turn Autocorrect off in the Typerighter app.
  • Text Replacements come from Settings › General › Keyboard › Text Replacement and expand when you type a space or punctuation.

Reporting a problem

The more specific the report, the faster it gets fixed. Useful things to include:

  • What you typed, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • The app you were typing in.
  • Whether Swipe Typing and Allow Full Access are on.
  • Your iOS version and iPhone model.

Privacy

Typerighter makes no network connections, and nothing you type leaves the keyboard. The full privacy policy has the details.
